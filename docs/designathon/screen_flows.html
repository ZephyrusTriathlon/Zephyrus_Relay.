Workflow overview

Twelve screens, one connected delivery record.

The main path moves one order from replenishment to receipt. Exception branches remain visible and return to the same order lifecycle.

Store | Order replenishmentChoose full cartons and place the order before 16:00.
Dispatcher | Confirmed order queueSelect confirmed orders and review service risk.
Dispatcher | Plan & validateAssign stops and vehicle, then check feasibility.
Loader | Loading manifestLoad in reverse stop order and check each shipment.
Driver | Next stopRecord arrival, exceptions, and delivery.
Driver | Proof of deliveryVerify cartons, recipient, and time.
Store | Receipt checkInspect the POD, confirm receipt, or report an issue.

Deferral branch

From the confirmed queue, Dispatch records a reason and impact, then Store sees the consequence.

Loading branch

A missing, damaged, or mismatched shipment pauses until the loader records a resolution.

Delivery branch

A failed stop stays outstanding for a return visit and never counts as a completed delivery.

Order replenishment

Purpose

Turn stock context into a confirmed next-day replenishment request.

Primary action

Use or adjust recommended cartons, review the order, then place it.

Important information

On hand, stock health, days of cover, suggestions, weight, window, cutoff, and depot.

Rationale

Stock health, full-carton suggestions and the 16:00 cutoff help a counter manager choose quantities quickly without a separate spreadsheet.

Store manager | Screen 02

Order tracking & ETA

Purpose

Give the outlet a dependable view of what Dispatch has planned.

Primary action

Track the order and open details when more context is needed.

Important information

Lifecycle, expected arrival, delivery window, route, vehicle, driver, and exception state.

Rationale

Order status, calculated arrival, delivery window and vehicle help the outlet prepare staff and space for the expected handoff.

Receipt check

Purpose

Separate driver delivery from the Store manager's confirmation of what arrived.

Primary action

Confirm receipt after checking cartons, or report an issue.

Important information

Delivered cartons, recipient, time, POD state, receipt confirmation, and issue record.

Rationale

Driver POD sits beside Confirm receipt and Report an issue, so the outlet can check cartons and record a discrepancy at the counter.

Dispatcher | Screen 04

Confirmed order queue

Purpose

Bring the orders that need a decision into one planning queue.

Primary action

Select an order, then assign it or record a deferral.

Important information

Outlet, window, cartons, weight, volume, temperature, access, last service, and repeat-skip risk.

Rationale

Orders, windows and service risk appear together so a planner at a large screen can choose the next assignment.

Relay | Dispatcher | Confirmed order queue

Dispatcher | Screen 05

Plan & validate

Purpose

Validate the route and selected vehicle before the manifest is released.

Primary action

Review all checks, confirm the plan, then release it to Bay 03.

Important information

Stop sequence, ETA, windows, distance, time, cartons, temperature, access, weight, volume, fuel, trips, and depot.

Rationale

Stop sequence, vehicle and seven checks expose an infeasible plan together on the dispatcher's large screen before release.

Dispatcher | Screen 06

Deferral decision

Purpose

Create a traceable decision when the current run cannot serve an order.

Primary action

Choose the reason, write the impact and next action, then record the deferral.

Important information

Order, outlet, window, days since service, previous skip, deferral reason, and protection for the next run.

Rationale

A reason and impact note make a skipped outlet visible and defensible at the planning desk when this run cannot serve it.

Dispatcher | Screen 07

Delivery & receipt follow-up

Purpose

Review completed delivery records and separate receipt follow-up from route completion.

Primary action

Open a delivered stop and inspect the driver POD and Store receipt issue.

Important information

Route completion, delivered time, recipient, outstanding delivery exception, receipt confirmation, and Store issue.

Rationale

The dispatch overview keeps delivered stops complete while identifying the order, outlet and receipt discrepancy for reconciliation.

Loader | Screen 08

Loading manifest

Purpose

Turn the released route into an unambiguous loading sequence.

Primary action

Check the current shipment and confirm it loaded before moving forward.

Important information

Vehicle, bay, driver, departure, temperature, stop number, order, products, cartons, and weight.

Rationale

Reverse stop order and carton counts guide fast loading on a shared warehouse tablet without burying the next shipment.

Relay | Loader | Loading manifest
Relay screen flows09 | Loader
Loader | Screen 09

Loading exception

Purpose

Stop a short, damaged, or mismatched shipment and preserve the reason.

Primary action

Select what happened, add useful detail, save the issue, and later record a resolution.

Important information

Order, exception type, affected products or cartons, dock guidance, and resolution note.

Rationale

A blocked shipment and resolution note let dock staff stop a short or damaged load before it leaves the bay.

Driver | Screen 10

Next stop

Purpose

Keep the driver's immediate route work understandable on a phone.

Primary action

Navigate or contact the Store, then record arrival when safely stopped.

Important information

Route progress, current outlet, window, address, order, cartons, weight, and connection state.

Rationale

The current stop, window, contact and next action stay prominent on a phone used only while safely stopped.

Relay | Driver | Next stop
Relay screen flows11 | Driver
Driver | Screen 11

Delivery exception

Purpose

Record a failed handoff without making the shipment look delivered.

Primary action

Select the problem, add detail, then save it for a later return.

Important information

Exception reason, contact or quantity detail, retained cartons, next step, and outstanding status.

Rationale

A reason, details and return-visit path keep an undelivered stop visible when access or goods prevent handoff.

Relay | Driver | Delivery exception

Driver | Screen 12

Proof of delivery

Purpose

Create the delivery record that Store and Dispatch can inspect.

Primary action

Verify the carton count, confirm the check, enter recipient and time, then complete delivery.

Important information

Expected cartons, handed-over cartons, verification, recipient, delivery time, and offline queue status.

Rationale

Carton verification, recipient and time create a clear delivery record on the phone for Store and Dispatch to inspect.

Relay | Driver | Proof of delivery
